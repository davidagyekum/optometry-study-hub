# Question blueprint

The bank contains 80 questions across six stable sections:

- Arterial origins: 12
- Ciliary circulation: 15
- Retinal circulation: 15
- Blood-retina barriers: 14
- Retinal microcirculation: 12
- Clinical vascular localization: 12

It contains 78 automatically assessed questions and two manual-only open responses. All ten supported formats are represented. Bloom coverage is 6 Remember, 18 Understand, 48 Apply, 6 Analyze, 1 Evaluate and 1 Create, giving 56 Apply-or-higher questions.

Difficulty is 20 Foundation, 42 Intermediate and 18 Advanced. Every one of the 18 objectives has at least two questions. Each question has a unique family, so the family maximum of two is satisfied without weakening selection diversity.
