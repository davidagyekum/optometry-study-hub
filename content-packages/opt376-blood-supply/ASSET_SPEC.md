# Assessment asset specification

Five original 1200×675 SVG diagrams support arterial origins, ciliary circulation, dual retinal supply, blood-retina barriers and retinal microcirculation.

Each diagram:

- uses neutral A-D markers without embedded answer text;
- includes an accessible SVG title and description;
- has normalized hotspot or label coordinates in the bank;
- remains legible at mobile and desktop widths;
- is copied byte-for-byte into the production asset directory.
