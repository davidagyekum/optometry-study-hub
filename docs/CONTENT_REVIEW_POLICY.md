# Content Review Policy

## Authority and scope

Lecture slides define the course boundary but are not automatically authoritative. New or revised clinical, anatomical, physiological, pharmacological, or pathological statements must be checked against an appropriate traceable source.

A lecturer name identifies the origin of supplied teaching material. It must not be presented as approval of rewritten notes, questions, corrections, or images unless that approval is documented.

## Question requirements

Every new assessment question must record:

- a stable question ID and version;
- its course, module, section, and learning objective;
- author and review status;
- at least one traceable source before reviewed or approved status;
- a locator such as slide, page, chapter, figure, or guideline section where available;
- an explanation and format-appropriate rationale;
- misconception tags when the question targets a known error.

Draft material must remain clearly separated from the live approved bank.

## Medical and optometric corrections

When lecture wording conflicts with a stronger source, preserve the curriculum context and add a reviewer note that records:

- the original claim;
- the corrected wording;
- the supporting source and locator;
- the reviewer and review date;
- whether the change affects examinable material.

Corrections must not be silently attributed to the original lecturer.

## Images and figures

Every instructional image requires:

- a source or creator record;
- a reuse-right or licence record where applicable;
- descriptive alternative text;
- a caption and credit;
- verification that labels are accurate and legible.

Do not add images with unclear ownership, unreadable labels, or unverifiable anatomy. Cropping and optimization must not remove clinically important context or attribution.

## Review statuses

- **Draft:** authored but not independently reviewed.
- **Reviewed:** checked by a named reviewer with sources and rationales complete.
- **Approved:** accepted for the live learning experience.
- **Retired:** preserved for history but excluded from production delivery.

Authors should not review their own intermediate or advanced questions unless an explicit exception is documented.

## Versioned campaign policy

`opt376-expert-review@1` requires three independent, unconflicted reviewers per applicable criterion, flags V below 0.80 for discussion, and treats ratings of 1 or 2 as concerns. Low factual-accuracy and image-rights ratings block readiness. Meeting the numeric policy never changes `reviewStatus`; human attribution and judgment remain mandatory.
