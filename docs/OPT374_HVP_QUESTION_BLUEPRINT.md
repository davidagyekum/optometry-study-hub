### Assembly rules

1. Use the explicit bank and exact current question versions.
2. Exclude `open_response` from the automatically scored 50-question session.
3. Apply the section and format quotas before filling difficulty targets.
4. Prefer at least 20 questions at Apply or Analyze level.
5. Do not include more than two questions from the same `familyId` in one session.
6. Do not include near-duplicate terminology questions in the same session.
7. Use deterministic seeded shuffling so a saved attempt can be resumed exactly.
8. Lock `diagnostic@1` for Study mode; matching, extended matching and image labels may receive component-level partial credit.
9. Preserve the existing legacy 50-question quiz as a fallback and keep its score history separate.
10. Present the curated route as slide-aligned study practice, not lecturer-approved examination content.
